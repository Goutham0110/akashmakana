How a booking runs

Three stages, no surprises on the night

Scope the room

One call to fix the date, the act, the venue and the budget. You get a single-page plan and a fixed quote within three working days.

Build the show

Artist contracts, stage and sound design, ticketing setup, permits, crew roster and a run sheet. You approve, we execute.

Run and hand back

Our team is on site from load-in to load-out. You get the photos, the settlement and a short what-worked note within a week.
